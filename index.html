About the Workshop

Intelligent Distributed Information Processing Systems

The rapid expansion of interconnected devices, IoT ecosystems, edge computing, and data-intensive applications has created a strong need for communication-efficient intelligent distributed information processing systems. This workshop focuses on frameworks that combine artificial intelligence, distributed computing, edge intelligence, and adaptive networking to optimize information exchange across geographically distributed and resource-constrained environments. Key areas include lightweight communication protocols, intelligent resource allocation, multi-agent systems, federated and decentralized learning, distributed data fusion, knowledge graphs, and digital twins with distributed intelligence.

The workshop addresses major challenges such as bandwidth and latency limitations, heterogeneous device capabilities, fault tolerance, energy-efficient processing, dynamic network conditions, and secure collaborative intelligence. It also emphasizes privacy-preserving computation by reducing unnecessary raw data transmission.

The discussions are motivated by the growing demand for real-time distributed applications in smart healthcare, autonomous transportation, industrial automation, smart grids, environmental monitoring, and next-generation wireless networks. By improving communication reliability and efficiency, the workshop aims to advance scalable, adaptive, and sustainable intelligent distributed systems aligned with emerging paradigms such as Edge AI, 6G networks, and large-scale cyber-physical systems.
